Privacy Policy

The International Industry, Institutions, Innovation & Research Consortium (I³RC) respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share with us through this website.

Information we collect

  • Details you provide voluntarily through our forms or messages — such as name, phone number, email, organisation, designation and areas of interest.
  • Information shared during membership, award nomination or event registration processes.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and process membership, nominations and registrations.
  • To share information about relevant events, programmes and opportunities.
  • To maintain records required for the administration of the consortium.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with partner institutions or event organisers only where necessary to deliver a service you have requested, or where required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

Terms & Conditions

  • Content on this website is provided for general information about the consortium, its activities and its programmes.
  • Fees, schedules, packages and programme details are subject to change. The confirmed terms communicated at the time of registration or application will apply.
  • Payments for membership, events or awards are governed by the specific terms communicated for each programme, including any refund or cancellation conditions.
  • Logos, names and materials of I³RC and partner organisations may not be used without prior written permission.
  • External links are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for the content of third-party websites.

Membership Rules

  • Membership is granted in the category matching the applicant's profile and is subject to verification and approval.
  • Membership becomes active on approval and receipt of the applicable fee.
  • Membership provides access to the consortium's network, activities and eligible opportunities; specific collaborations, consultancy assignments, international visits, publications, awards, funding and IP services are subject to availability, eligibility, applicable fees and the relevant approval or selection process.
  • Members must keep their contact and profile information up to date.
  • Members may not represent themselves as speaking on behalf of I³RC without written authorisation.
  • The consortium may suspend or withdraw membership for violation of these rules or the Code of Conduct.
  • Membership fees are subject to change from time to time, as determined by the consortium.

Code of Conduct

  • Integrity: Act honestly and ethically in research, collaboration and professional dealings.
  • Respect for IP: Respect the intellectual property, confidential information and contributions of others.
  • Professionalism: Treat all members, partners and participants with courtesy and respect, free from harassment or discrimination.
  • Transparency: Disclose conflicts of interest in collaborations, evaluations and award processes.
  • Accurate representation: Do not misrepresent membership, awards or affiliations, including as government accreditation or institutional endorsement unless formally authorised.
  • Responsible dissemination: Share research and innovation outcomes responsibly and with proper attribution.
