Key Benefits & Opportunities

Why you should participate

ISBN Conference Proceedings

Selected papers will be considered for publication in conference proceedings carrying an ISBN, with DOI allocation.

Scopus-Indexed Journal Opportunities

High-quality research papers will be recommended to relevant Scopus-indexed journals, subject to editorial screening.

Patent and Design Support

Eligible research outcomes may be considered for utility patent filing and/or design registration, subject to novelty and applicable legal requirements.

University Collaboration and MoU Opportunities

Participants may request consideration for academic and research collaboration through an MoU with Shinawatra University, subject to university approval and mutually agreed terms.

Best Paper Recognition

Outstanding papers will be considered for Best Paper recognition based on established evaluation criteria.

International Awards

Awards will be considered based on profile review and the selection criteria established by the Shinawatra University team.

Book Publishing Opportunities

Participants can explore book and monograph publication with ISBN at special concessional rates.

International Networking

Interact with international delegates, academic experts, researchers, innovators and industry professionals.
