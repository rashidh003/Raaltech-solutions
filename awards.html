Nomination & Eligibility

Nomination process

Who can nominate

Nominations may be submitted by individuals, institutions, industries, startups, MSMEs, and professional organisations.

Self or third-party

Applicants may nominate themselves or be nominated by an authorised representative or another eligible person.

Required documents

Nominations must include the required application form, professional profile, supporting documents, and evidence of achievements.

Committee evaluation

All nominations will be evaluated by the designated selection committee based on the published criteria for each award category.

Eligibility criteria

Industry & Corporate

Companies and industrial professionals demonstrating significant contributions to innovation, technology development, or industry–institution collaboration.

Academic & Institutional

Universities, colleges, institutional leaders, and faculty members demonstrating excellence in research, innovation, IP generation, or academic–industry engagement.

Research & Innovation

Researchers, scientists, inventors, and R&D teams with demonstrable research outcomes or innovative contributions.

Intellectual Property

Individuals or organisations contributing to IP creation, protection, management, technology transfer, or commercialisation.

Startup & MSME

Eligible startups, entrepreneurs, and MSMEs demonstrating innovation, technology development, business growth, or societal impact.

Student & Research Scholar

Eligible students and research scholars demonstrating creativity, research excellence, invention, or prototype development.

Lifetime Achievement & Special Recognition

Individuals with a sustained record of significant contributions to their respective fields.

Evaluation, Selection & Processing Fee

A fair and transparent process

  • Nominations will be assessed against the relevant award criteria, supporting evidence, originality, impact, and demonstrated achievements.
  • The selection committee's decisions will be based on the published evaluation process and applicable conflict-of-interest safeguards.
  • I³RC reserves the right not to confer an award in a category if nominations do not meet the required standards.
  • A nominal fee may be charged towards nomination processing, evaluation, and award administration, where applicable. The applicable fee and payment terms will be communicated to applicants in advance.

Important notes

  • Submission of a nomination does not guarantee selection.
  • Awards are subject to eligibility verification and the final decision of the designated selection committee.
  • Any nomination fee, if applicable, will be communicated transparently before submission.
  • Award decisions must not be represented as government accreditation or institutional endorsement unless formally authorised.
