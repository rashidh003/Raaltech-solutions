Activities of I³RC

Areas of work

Collaborate. Innovate. Protect. — from identifying industrial challenges to building a thriving innovation economy.

01

Industry Needs Identification and Analysis

  • Identify industrial challenges, technology requirements, and opportunities for innovation.
  • Connect industry needs with suitable research expertise and practical solutions.
02

Institutional Research Capability Mapping

  • Identify institutions, laboratories, faculty members, and research teams with relevant expertise.
  • Map institutional capabilities to address industrial problems and development needs.
03

Industry–Institution Collaboration

  • Facilitate partnerships between industries, universities, colleges, and research institutions.
  • Promote joint projects, consultancy, internships, prototype development, and technology transfer.
04

Research, Development and Innovation

  • Encourage applied research, product development, prototyping, testing, and validation.
  • Promote innovative solutions for industrial and societal challenges.
05

Intellectual Property Rights and Commercialisation

  • Promote awareness and support for patents, designs, trademarks, copyrights, and IP management.
  • Facilitate technology licensing, IP commercialisation, and industry adoption of innovations.
06

Consultancy and Technical Services & Funding Facilitation

  • Connect industries and institutions with technical experts, professional consultants, and domain specialists.
  • Facilitate technical assessments, feasibility studies, and industry-oriented problem-solving.
  • Identify relevant government funding schemes, grants, subsidies, incentives, and financial assistance programmes for industries, institutions, startups, and MSMEs.
  • Create awareness of government initiatives, innovation programmes, research grants, competitions, and entrepreneurship development schemes through events, seminars, and workshops.
  • Support eligible applicants in understanding scheme guidelines, preparing proposals, compiling documents, and submitting applications for funding opportunities and competitions.
  • Facilitate connections with relevant government departments, funding agencies, incubators, and other supporting organisations, where appropriate.
07

Faculty Development and Academic Advancement

  • Provide opportunities for faculty development programmes, expert lectures, and industry interactions.
  • Support faculty members in building professional profiles through research, IP, consultancy, and collaborative projects.
08

Startup, MSME and Entrepreneurial Support

  • Connect startups, MSMEs, and entrepreneurs with researchers, industries, mentors, and technical experts.
  • Facilitate innovation, product development, IP protection, and technology commercialisation.
09

Student Development and Skill Enhancement

  • Facilitate internships, industry exposure, research mentoring, and innovation projects.
  • Promote entrepreneurship, practical skills, and intellectual property awareness among students.
10

Conferences, Seminars and Knowledge Exchange

  • Organise national and international conferences, seminars, workshops, and expert forums.
  • Provide platforms for sharing research outcomes, industrial solutions, and emerging technologies.
11

International Collaboration and Global Exposure

  • Connect members with international industries, universities, research institutions, and innovation networks.
  • Provide global exposure through international partnerships, expert interactions, collaborative projects, and knowledge exchange.
12

Publications and Knowledge Resources

  • Encourage technical reports, research publications, case studies, white papers, and conference proceedings.
  • Facilitate knowledge sharing and responsible dissemination of research and innovation outcomes.
13

Awards, Recognition and Professional Networking

  • Recognise contributions to research, innovation, IP, entrepreneurship, and industry–institution collaboration.
  • Build professional networks among industrial leaders, academic heads, faculty members, researchers, and innovators.
14

National Development and Societal Impact

  • Encourage innovation addressing national priorities, community needs, and sustainable development.
  • Promote practical technology solutions that improve productivity, resource efficiency, and quality of life.
15

Economic Growth and Innovation Ecosystem Development

  • Support the conversion of research and innovative ideas into practical products, services, and business opportunities.
  • Contribute to entrepreneurship, MSME development, industrial competitiveness, employment generation, and national economic growth.
