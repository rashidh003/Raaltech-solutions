Who We Are

Bridging institutional knowledge and industrial needs

I³RC bridges the gap between institutional knowledge and industrial needs by encouraging meaningful partnerships, facilitating knowledge exchange, supporting technology development, and promoting the protection and commercialisation of intellectual property.

We believe innovation achieves greater impact when academic expertise, industrial experience, entrepreneurial vision and intellectual property strategies work together — creating opportunities for joint initiatives, industry-oriented research, prototype development, technology transfer and entrepreneurship.

What We Do

Services & initiatives that move ideas forward

From identifying industry needs to protecting and commercialising the resulting innovation, I³RC supports every stage of the journey.

Intellectual Property Rights

Awareness and support for patents, designs, trademarks, copyrights and IP management, along with technology licensing and commercialisation.

Industry–Institution Collaboration

Partnerships between industries, universities, colleges and research institutions for joint projects, consultancy, internships and technology transfer.

Research, Development & Innovation

Applied research, product development, prototyping, testing and validation to solve industrial and societal challenges.

Consultancy & Funding Facilitation

Technical experts, feasibility studies, and guidance on government schemes, grants, proposals and funding applications.

Startup & MSME Support

Connecting startups, MSMEs and entrepreneurs with researchers, mentors and experts for product development and IP protection.

International Collaboration

Global exposure through international partnerships, conferences, expert interactions, collaborative projects and knowledge exchange.
